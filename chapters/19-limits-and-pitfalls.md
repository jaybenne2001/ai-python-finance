# 19. Limits and pitfalls

Most impressive backtests are wrong for one of five reasons.

- **Look-ahead bias** — using information that was not available on the trade date (restated earnings, future index membership).
- **Survivorship bias** — testing only companies that still exist today, which drops the failures.
- **Overfitting** — tuning dozens of parameters until history looks great; out-of-sample results collapse.
- **Ignoring costs** — commissions, spreads, slippage and taxes can erase a strategy's entire edge.
- **Bad data** — missing days, unadjusted splits and stale prices from free sources; spot-check against a second source.

This material is educational, not investment advice. Models describe the past; markets change regimes.
